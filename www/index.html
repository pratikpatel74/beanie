Why Beanie

The Beanie changes every round

Round one the wild card is Aces. Round two it's 2s, all the way up to Kings in round 13. The same hand that wins one round can crumble the next — you can never get too comfortable.

Steal the Beanie

Spot an opponent using a Beanie wild card as a stand-in in their set? If you hold the real card it's covering for, you can steal the Beanie right out of their set and take it into your own hand. Watch the table turn in an instant.

Each round is short and punchy. One perfectly timed steal or a lucky draw can swing the whole leaderboard. Whether you're half a point up or trailing badly, you're always one round away from a comeback.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Open the link, enter a name, and you're in. No sign-up, no email, no password — ever.

How many players?

Beanie supports 2 to 4 players per game. You need at least two to start a room.

Is it free?

Yes — completely free to play in any browser. No ads, no in-app purchases. If you enjoy it, buy us a coffee to help keep the servers running.

How do turns work?

Each turn: draw a card (from the draw pile or top of the discard pile), optionally lay sets or add cards to sets already on the table, then discard one card to end your turn. Empty your hand to win the round and score zero. Full rules →

What counts as a valid set?

Two types — a Match (3 or more cards of the same rank, each a different suit) or a Run (3 or more consecutive cards of the same suit). Beanies can substitute for any card in either. Full rules →

How does scoring work?

The round winner scores 0. Everyone else scores penalty points for cards still in hand — face value for 2–9, 10 pts for tens and face cards, 1 pt for Aces, and 10 pts if you're caught holding a Beanie. Lowest total after all 13 rounds wins. Full rules →

Can I steal a Beanie from another player?

Yes. If a Beanie on the table is standing in for a specific card and you hold that exact card in your hand, you can swap yours in and take the Beanie for yourself. You must have already laid at least one set of your own first. Full rules →

What is the Score Tracker?

A free companion tool for tracking scores across all 13 rounds of a physical game of Beanie — useful when you're playing with real cards rather than online. Add up to 8 players, enter scores round by round, and it keeps running totals and highlights the round winner automatically. Open the Score Tracker →

Can I share the final scores with other players?

Yes. On the Game Over screen, tap Share result for a quick text summary of the final standings, or open the full scorecard and tap Share scorecard to generate and share a round-by-round image. On mobile it uses your native share sheet; on desktop it downloads as a PNG.
